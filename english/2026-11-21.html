오늘 대화 스크립트 · 고친 버전

18줄

실제로 주고받은 대화를 문법에 맞게 고친 것입니다. 굵은 부분이 오늘의 핵심 표현입니다.

A 피클 (선생님) B 정욱 님
A

Hi! It's Saturday morning. How's your day going so far?

B

So far, so good.

A

Nice to hear that! So, what are your plans for this Saturday?

B

Early this morning I went jogging. It takes about fifteen minutes, and then I walked about five kilometres.

A

Five kilometres — that's a solid start to the day! Do you do this every morning, or just on weekends?

B

Almost every day. I try to walk and run early every morning, before I start my day.

A

I understand you perfectly! So you exercise before your day starts — what time do you get up?

B

I get up at five twenty-five every morning.

A

Five twenty-five every morning — that's discipline! And how long have you kept this routine going?

B

More than six months.

A

That's a real habit now. Now, looking back: what was the best part of your week?

B

I had a business trip yesterday, Friday. I went to Gunsan, in Jeollabuk-do Province. I visited the manufacturer — my sourcing partner. They manufacture and supply parts for railway locomotives. Our company placed an order with them, and some of the products are still in production. The purpose of my visit was to check the current situation and discuss it with the CEO, the QA department, and a new person from the marketing and general affairs team. I made small talk with him, shared a few laughs, and heard about their situation. I want to understand where they are, so I can plan my next step.

A

So you checked production and read their situation for your strategy. Any surprises at the factory?

B

After the trip, I want to review what I learned this morning and this week, and practice it. Hearing something and being able to do it are not the same.

A

That's the right attitude — knowing and doing are different. What will you practice first?

B

First of all, I want to wrap up yesterday's business trip. I record my thoughts with a voice recorder and send them to Gemini, and it organizes and summarizes them. But if Gemini doesn't save them as a Google Doc on Drive, it's the same as garbage, because I can't find them when I need them.

A

Exactly — if it isn't saved as a Doc, it's useless. So how do you want to fix that?

B

I want to write a prompt so that Gemini makes a business trip report using the Google Docs stored in Drive. I'll do that today.

교정 포인트 · 원문 → 고친 문장

11개

들린 그대로 적은 문장과 고친 문장을 나란히 두었습니다. 틀린 곳만 눈으로 짚고, 고친 문장을 소리 내어 읽어 보세요.

원문 (들린 대로)

I walking and learning every early morning

고친 문장

I try to walk and run early every morning

설명

빈도 표현은 「early every morning」 순서. 동사는 try to + 동사원형

원문 (들린 대로)

before starting my day life, daytime

고친 문장

before I start my day

설명

day life / daytime은 어색. 「before I start my day」

원문 (들린 대로)

I get up at before 5:25 every

고친 문장

I get up at 5:25 every morning

설명

시각 앞에 before를 붙이지 않음

원문 (들린 대로)

I went to Bunsan city

고친 문장

I went to Busan

설명

부산 발음은 [부:-싼]

원문 (들린 대로)

Gunsan, 전북 province

고친 문장

Gunsan, in Jeollabuk-do Province

설명

도(道)는 「-do Province」

원문 (들린 대로)

we placed order to them

고친 문장

we placed an order with them

설명

place an order with + 회사

원문 (들린 대로)

Some product is ongoing, under process

고친 문장

Some products are still in production

설명

복수 products, 「in production」

원문 (들린 대로)

the purpose today call there was I checked~

고친 문장

The purpose of my visit was to check~

설명

목적은 「The purpose of ~ was to + 동사원형」

원문 (들린 대로)

say some small talking and making some laugh

고친 문장

I made small talk and shared a few laughs

설명

small talk는 make 동사와 함께

원문 (들린 대로)

고친 문장

설명

want 뒤에는 to부정사, 또는 need로 간단히

8개

오늘 대화에서 나온 것 중, 다음에도 바로 쓸 수 있는 표현만 골랐습니다.

  • 1

    half an hour

    30분

    an hour and a half

    1시간 30분 (1.5 hours)

  • 2

    make small talk

    가벼운 대화를 나누다

    × say small talk
  • 3

    place an order with ~

    ~에 발주하다

  • 4

    still in production

    아직 생산 중

  • 5

    knowing and doing are different

    아는 것과 하는 것은 다르다

  • 6

    read the situation

    상황을 파악하다

  • 7

    wrap up

    마무리하다

  • 8

    It's the same as garbage

    쓰레기나 마찬가지다
